What the forecast is worth

Two windows, same accounting

The group window is 55 days in late 2025; the long window is 229 days through October 2026. Same site, same battery, same wear charge. Bars are net operating cost, labels are savings against no battery.

Violations = power rating, simultaneous flow and state-of-charge breaches, counted per interval. Every controller scores zero.

Where the money comes from

Real Singapore prices are heavy-tailed. Half of every controller's saving is earned in the priciest 1% of intervals. The bar is split into that tail and everything else, so you can see how much of the result depends on rare events.

Method and limits

Everything on this page comes from one code base and one data panel. Nothing is a screenshot.

Data

  • Half-hourly settlement data from the Energy Market Company: uniform Singapore energy price, system demand, system solar output.
  • Irradiance, temperature and cloud cover from Open-Meteo; Henry Hub gas price from FRED when reachable, otherwise carried forward and flagged.

Site and accounting

  • 50 kWp rooftop array modelled from irradiance; 100 kWh battery rated 40 kW; state of charge held between 10% and 95%; 95% round-trip efficiency; SGD 0.012 per kWh of wear; 80 kW connection; exports paid at 85% of the import price.
  • Net cost = import payments - export revenue + wear charge. Energy left in the battery at the end of a window is credited at the price it could have been sold for, so a controller that finishes full is not punished.
  • Hard limits are enforced, not penalised: at most 20 kWh per interval in either direction, never both at once.

Controllers

  • Immediate self-use: PV serves load, surplus charges, deficits discharge. No price information.
  • Clock rule: charge 11:00-14:00, discharge 18:00-21:00. The midday-buy-evening-sell heuristic.
  • Price threshold: charge below the training 30th percentile, discharge above the 70th.
  • Four-rule mirror: the group pipeline's rule set, re-implemented so that the charge and discharge allowances share one power budget.
  • Quantile LP: rolling-horizon linear program over the next 16 hours, solved every half hour, constrained by the same physics.
  • Ex-post bound: one LP over the whole window with realised prices. Impossible to run, useful as a ceiling.

Limits

  1. The building load is a proxy: national demand shape rescaled to 181,648 kWh a year. No site meter exists.
  2. One site, one market. A regional comparison is future work.
  3. The quantile forecasts are miscalibrated at long horizons and are rescaled by a factor fitted on a validation window.
  4. The optimiser's terminal value was chosen on validation data only, and it is conservative for large batteries.
